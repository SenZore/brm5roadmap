🪂 SAWANG FINALE: Death From The Sky (MP5SD Route)

Difficulty: EXTREME | Giver: Ops Dir. R.M. Dulles | Reward: MP5SD + 2,500 Cr + 2,500 EXP + 5 Equipment

Safe 12-Mission Unlock Order (Dual Branch):

  1. First Step (Clear Nathon Town & HVTs)
  2. Midnight Rendezvous (CSEL radio call with Nick Pines)
  3. Positive Signal (Radio tower key + 120s broadcast defense)
  4. Enemy of my Enemy (Protect Santa Lilla mansion defectors 180s)
  5. Delivery Service (Drive supply truck past Docks road)
  6. Beach Holiday (Windy Gulf Hotel B001, B009, B107 photos + G002 laptop)
  7. [World Event] R&R Pharma Plant (Gas mask reactor shutdown)
  8. Aftermath (Escort Nick & Weiss to R&R Lab, hold 120s, grab hard drive)
  9. My House, My Rules (Stealth sabotage heli, arrest Richard Duke at Manor)
  10. Weiss Returns (Pick up Weiss near Trench Camp -> UNS Athens briefing)
  11. Attack on Sawang City (Dragon Hotel roof in <10 mins, confront Duke)
  12. Imposed Embargo (Hack Docks PC 90s, photo 4 containers, clear Docks)
  13. Optional/NamuWiki Branch: Sea Sick (Rescue Dr. Lakewell on SW cargo ship)
  14. Death From The Sky (Paradrop onto INSOLON San Antonio-class LPD!)

Tactical Walkthrough & Farming Meta:

  • Low Parachute Pull: Meet Nick Pines on the UNS Athens flight deck. Pull your parachute right above the stern deck of the INSOLON ship so deck AI cannot shoot you out of the sky.
  • 2-Minute Heli Warning: Push inside the hangar immediately—an enemy reinforcement helicopter lands troops on the rear deck ~2 minutes after touchdown.
  • 3 VIPs + Control Room AA: Use Frag Grenades (~30–40 for solo) and let Nick Pines (infinite HP) tank doorways. Kill the 3 VIPs (+500 Cr/EXP each), disable Anti-Air in the bridge control room, then reach the hostage.
  • 80s Extraction Holdout: Survive 80 seconds of infinite reinforcements, throw smoke on the deck, and board the extraction heli.

Naval Base Solo Tips:

  • Objective Persistence: Like all Ronograd compounds, completed sub-objectives at Naval Base remain saved even if the compound resets.
  • Underground Submarine Pen: Watch out for Smokers and shotgunners in the lower submarine repair dockyard.
